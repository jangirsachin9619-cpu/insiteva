Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

INSITEVA is an AI-powered business analytics application designed to help users analyze business data, discover insights, investigate problems, create visualizations, and generate reports.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an INSITEVA account, we may collect information such as your email address, account information, subscription information, and technical information required to operate the service.

2. Business Data

INSITEVA is designed to process business datasets such as Excel and CSV files.

Where applicable, dataset files are processed locally on the user's computer. INSITEVA's licensing and subscription systems may process account information, dataset identifiers or fingerprints, and usage metadata required to enforce subscription limits and provide the service.

INSITEVA does not require uploading an entire business dataset to the licensing system merely to verify a subscription.

3. Account Information

Account information may be used to authenticate users, maintain accounts, provide subscription access, and communicate important service information.

4. Payments

Paid subscriptions may be processed through third-party payment providers. Payment credentials such as card or banking details are handled by the applicable payment provider according to its own privacy and security policies.

5. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain INSITEVA.
  • Authenticate user accounts.
  • Manage subscriptions and entitlements.
  • Enforce dataset or usage limits.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse.
  • Improve reliability and performance.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect account and service information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Third-Party Services

INSITEVA may use third-party services for authentication, payment processing, hosting, infrastructure, analytics, or other necessary service functions.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain accounts, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, or deletion.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact INSITEVA support through the official support channel provided on the INSITEVA website.
